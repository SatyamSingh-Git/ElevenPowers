  could inherit a concrete compiler call target from a reusable signature.
  New controls failed; established callee/receiver binding now gates those edges.
- Pytest runtime-error prose mentioning `AssertionError` could enter the fault
  denominator. An actual ValueError producer reproduced it. Current classification
  checks the diagnostic boundary and actual Jest matcher records; mixed results
  stay incomplete.
- Missing Python executed-line context keys could mark partial attribution
  complete. The negative control failed; missing/empty attribution now yields
  no active observation edges.
- Corpus and machine-report limits were checked after full reads. Stream reads
  now stop at the limit plus one, retaining incomplete outcomes. Explicit
  evaluator JSON inputs are similarly bounded and sealed before launch.
