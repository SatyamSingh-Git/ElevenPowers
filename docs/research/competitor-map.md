## Reading the map

- The only systems with a real repository model are Aider and Continue. Everyone else greps.
- The only systems with code-enforced verification are gstack (evidence ledger, verify-gate), ECC (Stop typecheck), OpenCode (LSP diagnostics), and SWE-agent (lint-revert). None ties verification depth to task risk.
- The only systems with task-size triage are prose: ECC, BMAD, Superpowers.
- The only systems with memory that decays or is trust-gated are gstack and BMAD, and gstack reports its automatic capture does not fire.
- The strongest checkpointing is Cline's and OpenCode's; both are portable modules.
- Every process framework (Superpowers, ECC, Spec Kit, gstack, BMAD) enforces its process through text the model may ignore, and three of them say so in their own repositories.
- mini-SWE-agent's reported score with a 190-line loop and one tool is the lower bound any harness must beat to justify its existence.
